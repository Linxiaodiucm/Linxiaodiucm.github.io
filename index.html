Academic CV of Dr. Kai Zhou

Research Interests

  • The mathematical theory of kinetic equations, particularly of quantum Boltzmann equations;
  • The hydrodynamic limits of kinetic equations;
  • The mathematical theory of partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics.
